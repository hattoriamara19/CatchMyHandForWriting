📖 How to Use

  1. Put a bright green/yellow tape or marker on the pen tip.
  2. Press Start Camera.
  3. Keep the colored pen tip visible to the camera.
  4. Press Calibrate.
  5. Move the pen tip to the highlighted TOP-LEFT target and press Calibrate.
  6. Repeat for: TOP-RIGHT → BOTTOM-RIGHT → BOTTOM-LEFT.
  7. After calibration, move the pen tip inside the writing area.
  8. Move to the PEN-UP zone to stop the current stroke.
  9. Use Undo or Clear whenever required.
  10. Press Save PNG to save your writing.
💡 For best tracking, use a small bright green or yellow piece of tape/marker on the pen tip and use good lighting.
