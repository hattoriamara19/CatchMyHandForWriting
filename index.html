📖 How to Use

  1. Put a small bright green/yellow marker on the pen tip.
  2. Press Start Camera.
  3. Make sure the colored pen tip is visible.
  4. Press Calibrate.
  5. Move the pen tip to TOP-LEFT and press Calibrate.
  6. Move to TOP-RIGHT and press Calibrate.
  7. Move to BOTTOM-RIGHT and press Calibrate.
  8. Move to BOTTOM-LEFT and press Calibrate.
  9. Calibration will finish automatically.
  10. Move the pen to write in the air.
  11. Move the pen to the PEN-UP area to stop the current stroke.
  12. Use Undo, Clear and Save PNG when required.
💡 Best results: use a small bright green/yellow marker on the pen tip and good lighting.
